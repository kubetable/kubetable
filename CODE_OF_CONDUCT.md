# Code Of Conduct

KubeTable Community should be a practical, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior, and technical tradeoffs.
- Assume contributors have different backgrounds and constraints.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Publishing private information without consent
- Deliberately disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when behavior harms the project.
Security issues should be reported through the process in `SECURITY.md`, not discussed publicly first.
